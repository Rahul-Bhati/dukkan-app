To demonstrate the effectiveness of COEF-VQ, we deploy this new
framework onto the video management platform (VMP) at the
short-video platform, and perform a series of detailed experiments
on two in-house tasks related to video quality understanding. We
show that COEF-VQ leads to substantial performance gains from the
offline evaluation in these two tasks and effectively enhances plat-
form safety with limit resource consumption, significantly reducing
inappropriate content video view rate by 9.9% in a online A/B test
without affecting engagement. Post-launch monitoring confirmed
sustained improvements, validating its real-world impact.
